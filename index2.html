Questions & Guidance

Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful details on lead times, destination styling, and our sustainable practices.
How far in advance should we reserve our event date? +
We recommend booking weddings and large installations 6 to 12 months in advance to ensure harvest availability from our partner regenerative flower farms.
What does "foam-free floral design" mean? +
Traditional floral foam contains non-biodegradable microplastics. We use reusable chicken wire, kenzan pin frogs, and glass tubes that preserve fresh stems without harming water supplies.
Do you travel for destination events? +
Yes, we regularly produce celebrations across Europe, the Caribbean, and North America, sourcing from local botanical growers in the destination region.
What happens to the flowers after the event? +
Guests are welcome to take bouquets home in our recyclable wraps, or we donate remaining blooms to local senior centers and compost green waste.
