The Atelier Philosophy

Spaces That Breathe Silence and Stillness

Founded in SoHo, L'Atelier blends quiet French minimalism with tactile, organic materials. We reject sterile trends in favor of warm lime plasters, honed limestone, and aged oak that grow more beautiful with time.
